Help — how to read this

  • Topics are computed by clustering your actual comments — the top terms are shown so you can check the label.
  • Overlap = near-duplicate comments. A question asked 9 times is demand, not noise — answer it once, publicly.
  • Predictions are trend-based estimates from this data, always labelled ESTIMATE — never facts.
  • Under 20 comments the clusters are thin; the report says so when that happens.
